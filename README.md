# 📜 Terra-Ink | HyDE Project Theme

> *A tribute to ancient woodblock prints, parchment chronicles, and imperial ink art — engineered for modern Hyprland desktops.*

---

## 🏛️ English

**Terra-Ink** merges the timeless aesthetic of classical illustrated scrolls—feudal Japanese landscapes, Andean citadels, and Mediterranean shores—with cutting-edge desktop design. 

Built on a palette of warm parchment, deep obsidian ink, terracotta embers, and molten gold highlights, it delivers a high-contrast yet easy-on-the-eyes experience for long coding and creative sessions.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Sculpted Obsidian & Gold Waybar:** Custom-crafted floating capsules with continuous bronze rims, dedicated color-coded connectivity badges (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Audio, Battery), and illuminated active workspace indicators.
- **Warm Frosted Glass Aesthetic:** Deep obsidian transparency (`0.92` active / `0.85` inactive) paired with a 4-pass Gaussian blur, letting historic canvases breathe behind your windows.
- **Physics-Driven Spring Animations:** Natural deceleration curves, fluid pop-in effects, and smooth window sliding.
- **100% Isolated GTK3, GTK4 & Qt (Kvantum) Theming:** Dedicated styling for Thunar, Pavucontrol, Dolphin, and system context menus that strictly lives within Terra-Ink, leaving all your other HyDE themes untouched.
- **Hand-Curated High-Resolution Canvases:** Rich, textured illustration wallpapers designed to match every UI element seamlessly.
